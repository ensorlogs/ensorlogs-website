1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small files stored on your device when you visit a site. They remember preferences and provide aggregated usage information. Similar technologies such as localStorage may also be used.

2. Types of cookies we use

  • Technical (always active). Essential for the site to work, to remember light/dark theme, accessibility preferences and your cookie consent.
  • Analytics (optional). When you accept them, they help us understand which logs and projects perform best with aggregated data. If you decline, they are not loaded.
  • Marketing (optional). They would remember interactions with campaigns or CTAs. Today they are not loaded; use would be reserved for occasional collaborations and only with prior consent.

4. How to manage your consent

On your first visit you will see a notice with three options: Accept all, Reject all or Customize. You can change your choice anytime via the link in the site footer.
