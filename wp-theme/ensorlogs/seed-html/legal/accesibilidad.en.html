Accessibility statement

Ensorlogs is committed to making its digital content accessible according to WCAG 2.2 level AA and standard UNE-EN 301 549, in line with Directive (EU) 2016/2102 and Law 11/2023 (European Accessibility Act / EAA).

Measures implemented

  • Semantic HTML structure with header, main, article, section, nav, footer and role attributes where appropriate.
  • “Skip to main content” link visible on focus.
  • Enhanced visible focus on links, buttons and fields.
  • Accessibility toolbar: text size (3 levels), reading spacing, high contrast mode, saved in the browser.
  • Support for prefers-reduced-motion: animations are disabled if your system requests it.
  • Logs with progress bar, navigable table of contents (TOC), audience filters and author audio comment with custom controls.
  • Images with alt text and videos with captions when appropriate.
  • Minimum AA contrast verified for brand colours on light and dark backgrounds.

Content not fully accessible

Some imported or embedded third-party content (e.g. social networks, video embeds) may not meet all criteria. We are reviewing these cases to replace them with accessible alternatives or add transcripts / captions.

Found a barrier?

If you have difficulty accessing any content, email hola@ensorlogs.com with: the URL, a short description of the problem and, if possible, a screenshot. We will try to respond within a reasonable time and, in any case, within one month.

Compatibility and technologies

The site is tested on the latest stable versions of Chrome, Firefox, Safari and Edge on Windows, macOS, iOS and Android, with NVDA, VoiceOver and TalkBack screen readers.
